Solmar Bay — Streets of Gold is an original open-world crime game built with three.js. You play Jax Marlowe, fresh out and back in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar Bay, where fixer Mama Rosa always has work for you — and the rival crew The Vultures, led by Kade Voss, think they own Dockside. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel: steal cars, weave through traffic and pedestrians, fight back with a pistol, and outrun the Solmar Bay PD as your wanted level climbs from 0 to 5 stars. A live HUD with a minimap keeps you oriented while you work through three story missions — "The Pickup" ($500), "Hot Wheels" ($1,200), and "The Vault Job" ($5,000). Your money, missions, and earnings are saved in localStorage, so you can pick up where you left off. Everything here is original — no assets, characters, or code from Rockstar or GTA.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser and press Start.
